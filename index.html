Fit

Who this is for. And who it isn't.

A good fit

  • Know exactly who you sell to or have a rough idea
  • You sell through 1:1 conversations, not a checkout page
  • You're a founder or team wanting to push growth
  • Deals worth $15k+ a year
  • Your team spends hours finding accounts instead of talking to them

Not a fit

  • Self-serve, product-led products where no one talks to a buyer
  • Low-ticket offers where 1:1 outreach won't pay back
  • Someone who wants the emails sent for them.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Do you send the emails?

No. Cinzoro delivers the list and the briefs. That help you and your team close more deals.

What does it cost?

A one-time setup fee and a monthly retainer. Setup covers the build, testing and delivery of your system in one week. The retainer keeps it running and improving, with fresh verified contacts each month, updated scoring and a review of your results. Two signals are included, and more can be added. Exact pricing depends on your scope and is quoted on a short call.

How long does it take?

One week. We build your system, stress test it and hand it over ready to run. Once results start coming in, we use your feedback to keep improving it.

How do I know the emails are real?

Every email goes through a verification pass. Rows that come back empty or failed are removed before delivery.

What are signals?

Signals are the moments that show an account is ready to talk. Two come with every system: job changes, such as a new founder, head of sales or RevOps lead who is open to new tools, and hiring activity, such as a company building out its sales team. Your team reaches out when the timing is right instead of guessing. Need more? Add signals like funding, news, tech changes or buying intent.

What if my ICP isn't clear yet?

That's what the first call is for. We work out who actually buys from you, and who doesn't, before anything gets built.
